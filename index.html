      How this is computed

      The app pulls the most recent public posts from the account through the FxTwitter read API, trims them to a token budget, and sends them to a Groq-hosted Llama model with the prompt “read the tweets from this user, provide the political alignment of this user”. The model returns two axes in the range −1 to +1: economic (left to right) and social (libertarian to authoritarian). The marker is placed from those two numbers.

      This is a heuristic reading of recent public text, not a measurement of a person's beliefs. Retweets and quotes are included and counted as signal. Short samples, sarcasm, quote-tweets and non-political accounts all produce weak placements, which is what the confidence score is for.

      Runs entirely in your browser. Your Groq key is never sent anywhere except api.groq.com.
